# Seoul 2026 talk — working rules

## Writing and editing my text

- Preserve my choice of words and my structure wherever possible. Edit lightly: fix errors, tighten, add sources. Do not rewrite.
- Avoid the telltales of AI language: no "delve", "landscape", "crucial", "pivotal", "underscore", "navigate", "it's worth noting", no tidy triads, no "not X, but Y" flourishes, no summary sentence at the end of each paragraph.
- Jumps between paragraphs are fine. Not every paragraph needs a transition or a link to the one before it.
